BEFORE YOU TRUST A MODEL

Validation checklist

A strong score alone does not prove clinical usefulness.

  • ✓ Patient-level split where applicable
  • ✓ No train/test leakage
  • ✓ Class imbalance reviewed
  • ✓ False negatives investigated
  • ✓ Dataset limitations documented
  • ✓ External validation considered
Important

Clinical deployment requires rigorous validation, privacy and security controls, regulatory review, and qualified clinical oversight.

DATA WITH RESPONSIBILITY

Dataset & responsible AI

Start with public research data; never use identifiable patient images without appropriate authorization.

▧

Public dataset first

Consider a well-documented public chest X-ray dataset such as the Kermany et al. pediatric chest X-ray collection. Read its terms, provenance, and limitations before use.

Privacy by design

This front-end preview processes the selected image in your browser and does not transmit it. A future backend must add secure handling, access controls, retention policies, and audit logging.

Fairness & limitations

Dataset shifts, label quality, demographic imbalance, and acquisition differences can change performance. Do not generalize results without evidence.
